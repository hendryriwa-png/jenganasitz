Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · How JengaNasi collects, uses, and protects your data

1. Information We Collect

Account and contact details: full name, email address, phone number. Customer verification: national ID number when you choose to provide it. Vendor verification and payments: store name, store region, BRELA registration, business licence, national ID, TIN number, bank or mobile-money account details, and your store location (region or GPS). Orders: delivery address, order items, prices, and status. Usage data: pages visited and actions taken to keep the marketplace secure and improve it.

2. How We Use Your Information

To create and manage your account, process and deliver your orders, verify vendors (BRELA, licence, national ID, TIN), release payments and payouts to the correct bank or mobile-money account, provide customer support, send order updates, and comply with Tanzanian law and our legal obligations.

3. How We Store and Protect It

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored in secure, hosted databases protected by row-level access controls. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Your uploaded verification documents (BRELA, licence, national ID, TIN) are stored privately and are only reviewed by the platform administrator for verification purposes.

4. Who We Share It With

Vendors (to fulfil your orders and arrange delivery), payment providers (to process bank and mobile-money transactions), and government or law-enforcement bodies where required by law. Your personal data is never sold or rented to third parties for marketing.

5. How Long We Keep It

We keep your information while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards to comply with legal, tax, and dispute-resolution requirements. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time.

6. Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your personal information, withdraw consent, or ask questions about how your data is handled. Contact us at info@jenganasi.co.tz or +255 684 595 822 and we will respond within 7 days.

JengaNasi is an online marketplace that connects buyers with verified building-material vendors. The supply contract for each order is between the customer and the vendor; the platform facilitates the transaction but is not itself a party to the sale.

3. Vendor Obligations

Vendors must provide accurate product information, prices, and stock levels; deliver or arrange delivery of confirmed orders; honour verified returns and refunds; and pay the agreed marketplace site fee on each payout. Customers must provide accurate registration and delivery information, use the platform lawfully, and pay for confirmed orders through the available payment methods (mobile money, bank transfer, or cash on delivery where offered).

5. Payments & Payouts

Returns & Refunds

Incorrect or Damaged Items

If you receive goods that are damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, contact the vendor within 48 hours of delivery. Vendors are expected to replace or refund for verified issues.

Change of Mind

Returns for change of mind are at the vendor's discretion. Contact the vendor directly to discuss. Return delivery costs may apply.

Refund Process

Once a return is accepted, refunds are processed within 5–7 business days via the original payment method (mobile money or bank transfer).

Dispute Resolution

If you cannot resolve an issue with a vendor, contact us and we will mediate to reach a fair resolution.

Non-Returnable Items

Custom or special-order items, cut-to-size materials, and opened pack items cannot be returned unless defective. Check with the vendor before ordering.

Vendor Obligations

All vendors agree to our marketplace terms, including fair return and refund policies. Vendors failing to honour legitimate returns may be removed from the platform.

Delivery Information

Vendor-Arranged Delivery

Most vendors arrange their own delivery or recommend trusted logistics partners. Delivery timelines and costs are provided at order confirmation.

Pickup Option

Many vendors offer free pickup from their store or warehouse. Use this option to save on delivery costs if you have your own transport.

Delivery Timeline

Standard delivery within Dar es Salaam: 1–3 business days. Upcountry delivery: 3–7 business days depending on location and road conditions. Large or bulk orders may need additional planning.

Delivery Costs

Delivery fees are set by the vendor and depend on distance, order size and weight, and accessibility. Fees are clearly stated before you confirm your order.

Order Tracking

Once dispatched, vendors update the order status to "shipped" and may provide a tracking reference. Check your Orders page for the latest status.

Receiving Your Order

Inspect materials upon delivery. If items are damaged or incorrect, contact the vendor immediately through the platform. Do not accept damaged goods.
